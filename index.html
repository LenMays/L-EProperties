L & E Properties, LLC is a woman-owned construction company based in Jonesboro. What started with one lot more than twelve years ago has grown into a full-service residential and commercial builder, trusted with everything from single-family homes to multi-tenant retail spaces.

Behind every project is an in-house team of designers and contractors who carry the work from first sketch to final walkthrough, with one point of contact the whole way through.
